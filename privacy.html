Who uses this app

Clip Uploader is a private tool. Its only user is the owner of the Chat Went Wild channels, who uses it to upload video clips to their own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

What data the app accesses

  • Google / YouTube: the youtube.upload permission, used solely to upload videos and set their title, description and privacy status on the owner's channel. The app does not read, analyse or download any other YouTube or Google account data.
  • TikTok: the video.upload permission (and the basic profile information TikTok includes with login), used solely to send videos to the owner's TikTok drafts.

How the data is stored

The OAuth tokens that let the app upload are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never shared, sold, transferred or used for advertising, analytics or AI training. Uploaded videos are clips the owner has created and reviewed.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Clip Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time via Google Account permissions or TikTok's settings under Security → Manage app permissions. Revoking deletes the app's ability to upload; the locally stored tokens then stop working.
