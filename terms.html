Scope

Clip Uploader is a private tool operated by and for the owner of the Chat Went Wild channels. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Use

The app only uploads clips that the owner has reviewed and approved, to the owner's own YouTube channel and TikTok account, through the platforms' official APIs, and in line with the YouTube Terms of Service and TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

Content

Clips are short highlights from live streams, made for streamers' official clipping campaigns. Rights holders who want a clip removed can open an issue or use the platforms' own reporting tools; such requests are honoured promptly.
