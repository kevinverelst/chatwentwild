Chat Went Wild publishes short vertical clips of the moments that made a live stream's chat explode: clutch plays, fails, rage moments and pure chaos, cut down to the part that matters.

About the Clip Uploader app

"Clip Uploader" is a private tool used only by the owner of the Chat Went Wild channels. It uploads clips that the owner has reviewed and approved by hand to the owner's own YouTube channel and TikTok account, using the official YouTube Data API and TikTok Content Posting API. It does not access anyone else's account and is not offered to the public.

Clips are made for streamers' official paid clipping campaigns, where streamers invite clippers to share their stream highlights.
